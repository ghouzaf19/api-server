# ✅ PROMPT FOR REPLIT (Google December 2025 Update SEO Audit Fix)

You are a senior SEO + performance optimization engineer.

Your task is to directly audit and improve my existing blog website according to Google December 2025 update requirements.

Do NOT build new systems.
Do NOT create extra tools.
Only apply improvements directly to existing pages.

---

# 🎯 OBJECTIVE

Optimize the website for:

* Google Search ranking
* Google Discover performance
* Core Web Vitals (especially INP)
* Content authenticity and information gain
* Safe indexing (no penalties or manual actions)

---

# ⚙️ REQUIRED FIXES

## 1. Performance Gap Analysis (Search vs Discover)

* Identify pages performing well in Search but weak in Discover
* Improve:

  * visuals
  * headlines
  * engagement signals
* Optimize pages for Discover visibility

---

## 2. Authenticity & Information Gain

* Improve content to ensure:

  * real value (not generic AI text)
  * unique insights
  * practical experience
* Add:

  * real tips
  * mistakes people make
  * original explanations

---

## 3. Semantic Chunking Optimization

* Reformat content into clear blocks:

  * short sections
  * strong headings (H2/H3)
  * easy-to-extract passages
* Improve readability for AI + Google extraction

---

## 4. Visual Stability (INP Optimization)

* Improve interaction responsiveness (INP)
* Reduce layout shifts
* Optimize images and scripts
* Ensure smooth mobile experience

---

## 5. Citation Economy Optimization

* Add external authoritative references where needed
* Ensure citations are:

  * relevant
  * minimal but strong
* Avoid over-linking or low-quality sources

---

## 6. Content Refinement (Human + AI Editing)

* Improve content quality by:

  * removing repetition
  * improving clarity
  * enhancing readability
* Ensure final content feels human-written and trustworthy

---

## 7. Engagement Boost

* Add simple interactive elements where possible:

  * FAQ sections
  * tips boxes
  * comments prompts
* Increase user engagement signals

---

## 8. Security & Manual Action Safety

* Ensure:

  * no spam signals
  * no keyword stuffing
  * no thin content
* Make sure website is safe for Google indexing

---

# 🚀 FINAL GOAL

Transform existing blog into a:

* high-quality Google Search performer
* Discover-optimized content platform
* fast and stable (INP-friendly) website
* trusted and authentic content source

---

# ❗ IMPORTANT

* Do NOT rebuild the website
* Do NOT create new apps or tools
* Only improve existing pages and code
* Focus on SEO + performance + content quality
