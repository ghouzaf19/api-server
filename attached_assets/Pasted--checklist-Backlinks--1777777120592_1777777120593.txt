# ✅ PROMPT FOR REPLIT (SEO & BACKLINK STRATEGY – SAFE IMPLEMENTATION)

You are an SEO strategist specializing in ethical link building and Google-safe backlink growth.

Your task is NOT to spam links or automate outreach aggressively.

Your task is to build a **safe, sustainable backlink & authority strategy system** for my blog.

---

# 🎯 OBJECTIVE

Improve domain authority and organic traffic through:

* high-quality backlinks
* content-driven link earning
* digital PR strategies
* competitor analysis
* brand authority building

---

# ⚙️ IMPLEMENTATION TASKS

## 1. Backlink Profile Analysis

* Analyze existing referring domains
* Identify:

  * high authority links
  * low quality links
  * irrelevant links
* Suggest improvements

---

## 2. Anchor Text Optimization

* Analyze anchor text distribution
* Ensure natural mix:

  * branded anchors
  * generic anchors
  * keyword-based anchors
* Avoid over-optimization

---

## 3. Linkable Asset Strategy (CRITICAL)

Identify content types that naturally earn links:

* ultimate guides
* data-driven posts
* unique recipes or insights
* comparison articles

Generate ideas for link-worthy content.

---

## 4. Competitor Backlink Analysis

* Analyze competitors in same niche
* Identify where they get backlinks from:

  * blogs
  * forums
  * directories
  * media mentions
* Suggest replicable opportunities

---

## 5. Broken Link Building (SAFE VERSION)

* Identify opportunities where:

  * relevant sites have broken links
  * my content can replace them
* Suggest outreach targets

---

## 6. Digital PR Strategy

* Create ideas for:

  * shareable content
  * expert-style articles
  * press-worthy posts
* Focus on organic media mentions (not spam PR blasts)

---

## 7. Community & Forum Backlinks

* Suggest safe participation in:

  * niche forums
  * Reddit discussions
  * cooking/recipe communities
* Focus on value-first engagement (no spam posting)

---

## 8. Content Repurposing for Links

* Turn blog posts into:

  * guides
  * infographics
  * short articles
* Increase chances of natural backlink acquisition

---

## 9. Lost & Unlinked Mentions

* Detect opportunities where:

  * brand is mentioned without link
* Suggest outreach strategy to reclaim links

---

## 10. Local & Niche Citations

* Suggest relevant directory listings (if applicable)
* Focus on niche authority sites, not spam directories

---

# ❌ DO NOT DO

* No spam backlink generation
* No automated mass outreach
* No fake press releases
* No low-quality link farms
* No PBN strategies

---

# 🚀 FINAL GOAL

Build a **clean, Google-safe backlink strategy** that:

* improves authority over time
* attracts natural links
* strengthens SEO rankings
* avoids penalties or manual actions

---

# 🧠 RESULT EXPECTED

The system should output:

* backlink opportunities list
* content ideas for link building
* competitor insights
* safe outreach strategies
