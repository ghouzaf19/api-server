**PROMPT FOR REPLIT:**

You are an SEO optimization assistant working on a content website. Your task is to audit, improve, and implement the following pending SEO and E-E-A-T requirements across all blog pages.

### CONTEXT

The website already has basic content published. Your job is to upgrade it to meet modern SEO standards (Google Search + Discover) with strong emphasis on E-E-A-T, performance, and content quality.

---

## COMPLETED TASKS (already done)

No action needed for completed tasks.

---

## PENDING TASKS (must be implemented)

### 1. Author & Trust Signals (E-E-A-T)

* Create detailed author pages for each writer
* Add clear publisher transparency page (About / Editorial Policy)
* Ensure every article has:

  * Clear byline (author name)
  * Publication date + last updated date
* Add author bio sections showing expertise and experience

---

### 2. Content Quality & Authenticity

* Ensure content demonstrates first-hand experience (not generic AI text)
* Add original visuals where possible (real images, custom graphics)
* Avoid stock-only or repetitive visuals
* Improve headlines to be:

  * Honest
  * Clickable but not clickbait
  * SEO-optimized

---

### 3. External Authority & Credibility

* Add citations from authoritative sources when needed
* Link to trusted external references (official sites, studies, reputable blogs)
* Avoid low-quality or spammy outbound links

---

### 4. Content Strategy

* Mix:

  * Evergreen content (long-term SEO value)
  * Timely/trending content (for Discover traffic)
* Regularly refresh old content:

  * Update information
  * Improve SEO structure
  * Add new sections if needed

---

### 5. Visual Optimization

* Use large, high-quality images
* Enable image previews (OpenGraph / featured images)
* Ensure images are optimized (compressed, fast loading)

---

### 6. Technical SEO

* Optimize Core Web Vitals:

  * Improve page speed
  * Reduce layout shifts
  * Optimize mobile performance
* Implement structured data (Schema Markup):

  * Articles
  * Author
  * Breadcrumbs
  * Organization

---

### 7. Google Discover Optimization

* Ensure content is visually rich and engaging
* Use strong headlines + high-quality images
* Maintain freshness and regular updates
* Monitor performance using Google Search Console Discover report

---

## OUTPUT EXPECTATION

* Apply changes directly to the codebase
* Improve both frontend and SEO structure
* Keep design clean, fast, and mobile-friendly
* Prioritize Google SEO + Discover performance
