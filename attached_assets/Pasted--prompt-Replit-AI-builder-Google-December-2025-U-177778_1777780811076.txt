### **PROMPT (Advanced – Google Update Audit Fix)**

> I have a blog that needs to be fully optimized according to the Google December 2025 Helpful Content & Quality Updates.
>
> Here is my website: [PASTE YOUR BLOG LINK HERE]
>
> Apply a full audit and IMPLEMENT improvements based on the following checklist:
>
> ---
>
> ### 1. Performance Gap Analysis (Search vs Discover)
>
> * Identify why content may not perform in Google Discover
> * Improve emotional hooks, storytelling, and visual appeal
> * Add engaging intros and scroll-stopping headlines
>
> ---
>
> ### 2. Authenticity & Information Gain
>
> * Rewrite content to be more original and helpful
> * Add unique insights, tips, or personal-style commentary
> * Avoid generic AI-style writing
>
> ---
>
> ### 3. Semantic Chunking Optimization
>
> * Break content into clear sections with optimized H2/H3 headings
> * Use bullet points, short paragraphs, and structured layout
> * Improve readability for both users and SEO
>
> ---
>
> ### 4. Core Web Vitals / INP Optimization
>
> * Improve page speed and responsiveness
> * Optimize images (lazy loading, compression)
> * Reduce layout shifts and improve mobile UX
>
> ---
>
> ### 5. Optimize for Citation Economy (EEAT)
>
> * Add credible references and useful outbound links
> * Strengthen authority and trust signals
> * Add author credibility and about section if missing
>
> ---
>
> ### 6. Content Perfection (AI + Human Style)
>
> * Rewrite content to feel natural, human, and engaging
> * Improve clarity, tone, and flow
> * Make content actionable and practical
>
> ---
>
> ### 7. Engagement Boost
>
> * Add interactive elements:
>
>   * FAQs
>   * Tips boxes
>   * “Did you know?” sections
>   * Call-to-actions
>
> ---
>
> ### 8. Security & Manual Actions
>
> * Ensure no spammy practices
> * Clean up broken links
> * Ensure site is safe and compliant with Google policies
>
> ---
>
> ### FINAL TASK:
>
> * Apply all improvements directly to my blog structure and content
> * Suggest a new homepage layout
> * Improve at least 1 blog post as a full example
> * Provide a list of SEO keywords for my niche (meat recipes / carnivore diet)
>
> Make everything optimized for:
>
> * Google rankings
> * Google Discover
> * Pinterest traffic
> * Monetization (ads + affiliate)
