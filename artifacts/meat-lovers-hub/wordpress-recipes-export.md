The Ultimate Slow Cooker Pulled Pork (Fork-Tender in 8 Hours)

### Intro (paste as first paragraph block in Gutenberg)

If you've never made slow cooker pulled pork, you're about to change your weekend plans. This recipe needs just 6 pantry ingredients, takes 15 minutes to prep, and comes out impossibly tender every single time. The hardest part? Waiting 8 hours while your house fills with the most incredible BBQ smell imaginable.

---

### Ingredients

**Dry Rub:**
- 1.5 kg (3 lb) pork shoulder (bone-in)
- 2 tbsp brown sugar
- 1 tbsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 1.5 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp cayenne pepper (optional)

**For the slow cooker:**
- 1 cup BBQ sauce (store-bought or homemade)
- 1/2 cup apple cider vinegar
- 1/2 cup chicken broth

**To serve:**
- Brioche buns
- Coleslaw
- Pickled jalapeños

---

### Instructions

1. **Mix the dry rub.** Combine brown sugar, smoked paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, cumin, salt, pepper, and cayenne in a small bowl.
2. **Coat the pork.** Pat the pork shoulder completely dry with paper towels, then rub the spice mixture all over every surface — don't miss the underside.
3. **Sear for depth of flavour (optional but recommended).** Heat a splash of oil in a large heavy pan over high heat. Sear the pork on all sides for 2–3 minutes per side until a deep brown crust forms. This step adds enormous flavour.
4. **Load the slow cooker.** Pour BBQ sauce, apple cider vinegar, and chicken broth into the slow cooker. Add the pork shoulder fat-side up.
5. **Cook low and slow.** Cover and cook on LOW for 8–10 hours (or HIGH for 5–6 hours) until the meat shreds easily with two forks.
6. **Shred the pork.** Remove the pork to a large bowl and shred with two forks. Discard the bone. Skim fat off the remaining slow cooker juices.
7. **Mix it back together.** Return shredded pork to the slow cooker. Stir to coat in all those amazing juices. Taste and adjust seasoning.
8. **Serve.** Load onto toasted brioche buns with crunchy coleslaw and pickled jalapeños.

---

### Chef's Tips

- **Make it ahead:** Pulled pork keeps in the fridge for 4 days and freezes perfectly for up to 3 months.
- **Don't skip the sear:** Even 3 minutes of high-heat searing adds a deeper, more complex flavour.
- **Bone-in is better:** Bone-in pork shoulder stays moister than boneless during the long cook.

Sticky Honey Garlic Chicken Thighs — 30-Minute One-Pan Dinner

### Intro

Six ingredients. One pan. Thirty minutes. That's the entire pitch for these honey garlic chicken thighs, and I'll be honest — they don't need any more selling than that. The sauce is sticky, glossy, a little sweet, a little garlicky, and it caramelises on the chicken in a way that makes you want to lick the pan. These go on the weekly rotation immediately.

---

### Ingredients

- 8 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs
- Salt and black pepper
- 1 tbsp olive oil

**Honey Garlic Sauce:**
- 5 cloves garlic (minced)
- 1/3 cup honey
- 3 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
- 1 tsp sesame oil (optional)

**To garnish:**
- Sesame seeds
- Sliced spring onions

---

### Instructions

1. **Season the chicken.** Pat the chicken thighs completely dry. Season generously with salt and black pepper on both sides.
2. **Sear skin-side down.** Heat olive oil in a large oven-safe skillet over medium-high heat. Add chicken thighs skin-side down and cook without moving them for 7–8 minutes until the skin is deep golden and crispy.
3. **Flip and cook the underside.** Flip and cook for 3 minutes more. Remove chicken and set aside temporarily.
4. **Make the sauce.** In the same pan, reduce heat to medium. Add minced garlic and cook for 30 seconds until fragrant. Add honey, soy sauce, and apple cider vinegar. Stir and let the sauce bubble for 1–2 minutes until it thickens slightly.
5. **Return the chicken.** Nestle the chicken thighs back into the pan, skin-side up, and spoon sauce over them.
6. **Finish in the oven.** Transfer the pan to a preheated 200°C (390°F) oven and roast for 15–18 minutes until the chicken is cooked through (internal temp 74°C / 165°F) and the sauce is sticky and caramelised.
7. **Garnish and serve.** Spoon pan juices over the chicken, scatter with sesame seeds and spring onions. Serve immediately over steamed rice.

---

### Chef's Tips

- **Don't move the chicken while searing** — that's how you get crispy skin.
- **Sauce too thin?** Let it bubble a minute longer before adding the chicken back.
- **Boneless works too** — just reduce oven time to 10–12 minutes.

Authentic Korean Beef Bulgogi — Better Than Takeout & Ready in 30 Minutes

**WordPress Category:** Beef

**WordPress Slug:** /recipes/korean-beef-bulgogi
**Author:** Chef Marco

**Cook Time:** 30 min (+ 1 hr marinating) | **Prep Time:** 15 min | **Difficulty:** Easy | **Serves:** 4

---

### Intro

Bulgogi — which literally means "fire meat" in Korean — is one of the most beloved dishes in Korean cuisine, and for good reason. Thin slices of beef are marinated in a sweet, savoury sauce, then seared quickly over high heat until the edges caramelise. The result is tender, juicy, slightly smoky, and absolutely addictive. This recipe comes together in 30 minutes (plus an easy marinade) and tastes better than most Korean restaurants.

---

### Ingredients

**For the marinade:**
- 700g (1.5 lb) beef ribeye or sirloin, very thinly sliced
- 5 tbsp soy sauce
- 3 tbsp brown sugar
- 2 tbsp sesame oil
- 5 cloves garlic (minced)
- 1 tsp fresh ginger (grated)
- 2 tbsp rice wine or dry sherry
- 1 Asian pear (grated) — OR 1/2 kiwi fruit (the enzyme tenderises the meat)
- 1/2 tsp black pepper

**For cooking:**
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 1 white onion (thinly sliced)
- 2 spring onions (sliced)

**To serve:**
- Steamed rice
- Lettuce leaves (for wraps)
- Kimchi
- Sesame seeds

---

### Instructions

1. **Slice the beef thin.** For easiest slicing, freeze the beef for 30 minutes first. Cut against the grain into slices no thicker than 3mm (1/8 inch).
2. **Make the marinade.** In a large bowl, whisk together soy sauce, brown sugar, sesame oil, garlic, ginger, rice wine, grated pear, and black pepper until the sugar dissolves.
3. **Marinate the beef.** Add the sliced beef and onion slices to the marinade. Toss to coat every piece thoroughly. Marinate for at least 30 minutes at room temperature, or up to 4 hours in the fridge. Longer = more flavour.
4. **Get the pan ripping hot.** Heat a large skillet, wok, or grill pan over the highest heat possible. Add oil and let it smoke.
5. **Cook in batches.** Add beef in a single layer — do NOT crowd the pan or it will steam instead of sear. Cook for 2–3 minutes without touching, then toss for 1–2 minutes more until caramelised and just cooked through.
6. **Repeat.** Cook remaining beef in batches the same way.
7. **Serve immediately.** Over steamed rice or in butter lettuce leaves as wraps, topped with spring onions, sesame seeds, and a generous pile of kimchi.

---

### Chef's Tips

- **The grated pear is key.** Enzymes in Asian pear or kiwi break down the beef proteins, giving you that restaurant-tender texture.
- **High heat is non-negotiable.** Low heat means steamed, grey beef. Hot heat means caramelised, flavourful bulgogi.
- **Make extra marinade.** It doubles as a stir-fry sauce for vegetables.

Perfect Pan-Seared Lamb Chops with Rosemary Garlic Butter (20 Minutes)

**WordPress Category:** Beef
**WordPress Tags:** lamb chops, pan seared, rosemary, garlic butter, date night dinner, quick dinner, lamb recipes
**WordPress Slug:** /recipes/pan-seared-lamb-chops-rosemary-garlic-butter
**Author:** Chef Marco
**Publish Date:** 2024-05-02
**Cook Time:** 20 min | **Prep Time:** 5 min | **Difficulty:** Medium | **Serves:** 2

---

### Intro

Lamb chops are one of the most impressive things you can put on a dinner plate, yet they're genuinely one of the quickest proteins to cook. The window between raw and overcooked is generous, the flavour is extraordinary, and a simple rosemary garlic butter transforms them into something restaurant-worthy in under 20 minutes. This is the recipe for when you want to genuinely impress — without spending hours in the kitchen.

---

### Ingredients

- 6 lamb loin chops (about 2.5cm / 1 inch thick)
- Salt and freshly cracked black pepper
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 3 tbsp unsalted butter
- 4 cloves garlic (lightly crushed, skin on)
- 3 sprigs fresh rosemary
- 2 sprigs fresh thyme
- 1 lemon (zested and halved, for serving)

---

### Instructions

1. **Bring lamb to room temperature.** Remove chops from the fridge 20–30 minutes before cooking. Pat completely dry — moisture is the enemy of a good sear.
2. **Season generously.** Season all surfaces heavily with salt and freshly cracked black pepper. Don't be shy — this is the only seasoning going into the meat.
3. **Get your pan screaming hot.** Heat a heavy cast iron or stainless steel skillet over high heat for 2–3 minutes. Add olive oil and let it ripple and just begin to smoke.
4. **Sear the first side.** Add lamb chops and press gently to ensure full contact with the pan. Sear for 3–4 minutes without moving until a deep mahogany crust forms.
5. **Flip and add butter aromatics.** Flip the chops. Immediately add butter, garlic cloves, rosemary, and thyme to the pan. As the butter melts and foams, tilt the pan and baste the chops continuously with the herb butter for 2–3 minutes.
6. **Check doneness.** For medium-rare, cook to an internal temperature of 57–60°C (135–140°F). For medium, 63°C (145°F). Remove from heat when 3°C below target — carry-over cooking does the rest.
7. **Rest.** Transfer chops to a warm plate and rest for 5 minutes. Do not skip this — it makes or breaks the juiciness.
8. **Serve.** Plate the chops, drizzle with the remaining herb butter from the pan, finish with lemon zest and a squeeze of lemon.

---

### Chef's Tips

- **Dry is critical.** Any moisture on the surface steams instead of searing. Pat dry, dry, dry.
- **Don't move the chops** while searing. The crust needs time to form — if it sticks, it's not ready to flip.
- **Lemon brightens everything.** The squeeze of lemon at the end cuts through the richness and makes all the flavours pop.

The Ultimate Homemade BBQ Bacon Cheeseburger — Smoky, Juicy & Loaded

**SEO / Yoast Meta Title (≤60 chars):**

**SEO / Yoast Meta Description (≤160 chars):**
Skip the drive-through — this homemade BBQ bacon cheeseburger beats any fast food burger. Smoky, juicy, loaded with flavour, and ready in 25 minutes.

**WordPress Category:** Quick Meals
**WordPress Tags:** burger, bacon cheeseburger, BBQ burger, homemade burger, easy burger recipe, beef patty, quick dinner
**WordPress Slug:** /recipes/bbq-bacon-cheeseburger
**Author:** Chef Marco
**Publish Date:** 2024-05-09
**Cook Time:** 25 min | **Prep Time:** 10 min | **Difficulty:** Easy | **Serves:** 4

---

### Intro

The difference between a fast food burger and a truly great homemade burger comes down to three things: the fat content of the beef, the heat of the pan, and a little patience. This BBQ bacon cheeseburger gets all three right. Smoky bacon, melted cheddar, tangy BBQ sauce, and a perfectly seared patty on a toasted brioche bun — it's the kind of burger that ruins fast food forever.

---

### Ingredients

**The patties:**
- 700g (1.5 lb) ground beef, 80/20 fat ratio
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
- Salt and black pepper

**The build:**
- 8 rashers smoked streaky bacon
- 4 slices mature cheddar cheese
- 4 brioche burger buns
- Butter (for toasting buns)
- 4 tbsp BBQ sauce
- 2 tbsp mayonnaise
- Iceberg lettuce
- 2 beef tomatoes (sliced thick)
- 1 red onion (thinly sliced)
- Sliced dill pickles

---

### Instructions

1. **Cook the bacon.** Lay bacon rashers in a cold skillet, then bring to medium heat. Cook 4–5 minutes per side until crispy. Remove and drain on paper towels. Keep the bacon fat in the pan.
2. **Form the patties.** In a large bowl, gently combine ground beef with garlic powder, onion powder, smoked paprika, Worcestershire sauce, salt, and pepper. Do not overwork the meat — mix just until combined. Divide into 4 equal balls, then press into patties about 1.5cm (3/4 inch) thick. Make a small thumbprint indent in the centre of each patty — this prevents doming.
3. **Get the pan very hot.** Bring the skillet with bacon fat to high heat until just smoking.
4. **Sear the patties.** Cook patties for 3–4 minutes per side for medium, pressing down slightly only for the first 10 seconds to ensure contact. Do not press again — you'll squeeze out all the juices.
5. **Add cheese and melt.** In the last minute of cooking, place a cheddar slice on each patty. Cover the pan with a lid for 45–60 seconds until the cheese melts perfectly.
6. **Toast the buns.** Butter the cut sides of the brioche buns and toast in a hot pan for 1–2 minutes until golden and slightly crisp.
7. **Build the burger.** Spread BBQ sauce on the bottom bun, then mayo on the top. Stack: bottom bun → patty with melted cheese → 2 bacon rashers → tomato → red onion → pickles → lettuce → top bun. The sauce goes at the base so it doesn't make the top soggy.
8. **Serve immediately** — burgers don't wait for anyone.

---

### Chef's Tips

- **80/20 beef is non-negotiable.** Leaner beef makes a dry, tough patty. Fat = flavour and moisture.
- **Cold meat, hot pan.** Keep patties cold until they hit the pan — it helps form a better crust.
- **The thumbprint trick** stops the patty puffing up in the middle, giving you a flat, even burger every time.
- **Build order matters.** Sauce on the bottom bun protects the bun from absorbing juices. Lettuce at the top creates a moisture barrier on the top bun.
